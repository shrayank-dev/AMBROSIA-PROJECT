# Code of Conduct

AMBROSIA PROJECT is a technical research community. Participants must be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussions focused on reproducible engineering.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential sharing, submission of proprietary or unlawfully obtained material, and instructions to bypass access controls or platform protections.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, the project, or third-party rights. Report conduct concerns privately through the security reporting channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
